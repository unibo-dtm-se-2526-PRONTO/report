## Abstract

“Pronto” is a web application that takes the place of the telephone helpdesk at the Cesena Campus of the University of Bologna. Students write their question in their own words and first receive the most relevant answer from a collection of real past questions. When that is not enough, they can book an appointment with the right office, choosing among the slots its staff have made available. Each booking goes to a free employee, so the workload stays balanced, and an e-mail confirms every booking and cancellation.

The application is built with Django and Vue.js, and finds answers by combining keyword search with semantic search.
